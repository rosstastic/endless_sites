About Us

Columbia Nails

Columbia Nails has been a trusted neighborhood nail salon in Columbia's Hickory Ridge community for years. Our skilled technicians — including Vivian, Ann, and Katie — deliver meticulous manicures, luxurious pedicures, and expert nail enhancements in a clean, relaxing environment. Whether you're stopping in for a quick polish change or a full spa pedicure, you'll leave looking and feeling your best.
