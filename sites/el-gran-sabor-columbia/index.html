About

El Gran Sabor

El Gran Sabor is a family-run Salvadoran and Mexican kitchen tucked into Oakland Mills. The hand-pressed pupusas — filled with chicharrón, cheese, or loroco — are made fresh to order. Fajitas arrive on a sizzling cast iron plate, the tacos come loaded and generously portioned, and the homemade chips and salsa are worth the trip alone. Everything tastes like it was cooked by someone who loves food. Because it was.
