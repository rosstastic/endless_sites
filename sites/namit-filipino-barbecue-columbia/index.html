Our Story

Namit Filipino Barbecue

Namit Filipino Barbecue brings the bold, smoky flavors of the Philippines to Columbia, Maryland. Founded by Maria Tabligan and Paolo Peñalosa — a family from Talisay, Cebu — Namit started as a beloved food truck and is now opening Howard County's only Filipino dine-in restaurant. Every skewer, every plate of sisig, every lumpia is made from cherished family recipes passed down through generations.

Serving Columbia since 2021
