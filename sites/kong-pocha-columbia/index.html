Our Story

Kong Pocha

Kong Pocha is Columbia's late-night Korean comfort food spot — a rustic-modern eatery with a retro 70s vibe where the fried chicken is legendary and the soju flows freely. Started by Sobin Kang, who built a cult following at the original Baltimore location on W. 20th St, the Columbia outpost brings the same home-cooked Korean fare — kimchi pancakes, spicy rice cakes, smoky BBQ, and crispy chicken in half a dozen styles — to Howard County. Open until 1am most nights. Walk-ins welcome.
