About Us

Merriweather Cafe & Deli

Merriweather Cafe & Deli is a beloved Columbia gem nestled in the heart of the Merriweather District. Run by Mrs. Connie, this neighborhood deli serves up fresh-made sandwiches, quality coffee, and the kind of genuine hospitality you simply can't find at a chain. Regulars make the trip every morning just for the breakfast sandwiches and the welcoming atmosphere.
